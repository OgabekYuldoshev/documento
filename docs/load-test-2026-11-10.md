## 2. Qisqa xulosa

1. **PDF generatsiya**: oddiy hujjat (10-200 qator) uchun barqaror **~150-175 hujjat/s** (~10-15 mln/kun), to'liq 10 yadro bilan. Bitta so'rov latency ~105 ms.
2. **O'qish endpointlari**: 2 500-3 400 req/s (bitta Node jarayon, bitta yadro 100% da to'yinadi).
3. **Sinish nuqtalari** (3 ta, 5-bo'limda batafsil):
   - 200+ bir vaqtdagi yangi ulanishda `ECONNRESET` boshlanadi (o'qishda ham).
   - Navbat chuqur bo'lsa (>1000 kutayotgan job) `claimNext` so'rovi kvadratik sekinlashadi: 5000 job 87 dan 19 hujjat/s ga tushdi.
   - Katta payload (60 000 qator, 3 MB) 4 parallel renderda **3.8 GB RAM** yeydi.
4. Rate limit, 413 (katta fayl), idempotentlik, navbat to'g'ri ishladi: yo'qolgan yoki buzilgan hujjat **0**.

## 5. Stress: sinish nuqtalari

### 5.1 Navbat (async, `Prefer: respond-async`)

| Navbatga qo'yildi | Qabul tezligi | To'liq tugash | O'rtacha | Natija |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 000 | 952/s (1.1 s) | 11.4 s | **87.6 hujjat/s** | 1000 ok |
| 5 000 | 856/s (5.9 s) | 257.8 s | **19.4 hujjat/s** | 5000 ok |

Hech narsa yo'qolmadi, hammasi `ok`. Lekin chuqur navbatda throughput 4.5x tushdi.

**Sabab (o'lchandi)**: `generations.repository.ts` `claimNext` har claim da `ORDER BY (select count(*) ... running ...)` korrelyatsiyalangan subquery bilan **barcha** `queued` qatorlarni skanerlaydi, va subquery project bo'yicha butun tarixni o'qiydi (`generation_project_idx` da `status` yo'q). Alohida SQLite da vaqt:

| Navbatdagi job | Bitta claim vaqti |
|---|---|
| 1 000 | 47 ms |
| 5 000 | 138 ms |
| 20 000 | **950 ms** (tarix ham o'sadi, yomonlashadi) |

Claim bir vaqtda 4 ta ishchi uchun ketma-ket, shuning uchun 20 000 navbatda throughput ~1 job/s gacha tushishi kutiladi. Tavsiya: (a) `(project_id, status)` indeks, (b) per-project running sonini subquery o'rniga bitta guruhlangan so'rov yoki oldindan hisoblangan jadval bilan olish, (c) `PROJECT_MAX_PENDING` ni real qiymatga (default 100) qo'yish; sinovda 100 000 qilindi, aynan shu himoyani o'chirdi.

## 8. Tavsiyalar (ustuvorlik bo'yicha)

1. **`claimNext` ni tuzatish**: indeks `(project_id, status)` va korrelyatsiyalangan subquery ni yo'qotish. Eng katta real xavf: navbat to'lganda butun tizim sekinlashadi.
2. **Sinxron kutish polling ini** 100 ms dan ~10-20 ms ga (yoki event) tushirish: latency 105 -> ~40 ms.
3. `PROJECT_MAX_PENDING` ni production da past qoldirish (default 100); global navbat chegarasi qo'shish (hozir faqat project bo'yicha).
4. `projects.list` ga sahifalash (superadmin 200 project da 68 KB, 560 req/s).
5. Katta payloadlar uchun: `maxPayloadKb` ni shablon bo'yicha real qiymatga tushirish (hozir default 10 MB gacha ruxsat, 3 MB payload 3.8 GB RAM yedi) va Linux da `prlimit` ishlashini tekshirish.
6. Production (Docker + Caddy) da 5.2 ni takrorlash; `ECONNRESET` chegarasini aniqlash, kerak bo'lsa Node `server.listen` backlog va `keepAliveTimeout` ni sozlash.
7. Kelajakda: PDF saqlash uchun S3 (storage interfeysi tayyor), disk monitoringi.
